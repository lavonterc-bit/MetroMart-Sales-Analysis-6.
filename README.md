MetroMart Sales Performance Analysis

Tools: Microsoft Excel, XLOOKUP, PivotTables, Excel formulas

Project Overview

This beginner-level data analytics project examines simulated MetroMart sales transactions from April through September 2026 to identify revenue trends, product performance, and regional sales fluctuations.

Business Problem

MetroMart management wants to understand changes in revenue, particularly the decline between August and September, and identify areas requiring further investigation.

Data Preparation

Inspected 72 sales records.

Investigated missing quantities, duplicate transaction IDs, returns, and promotional giveaways.

Used XLOOKUP to connect product IDs to product names.

Calculated transaction revenue and created PivotTables.

Key Findings

Total recorded revenue: $5,701.48.

Revenue declined by $243.05 (22.2%) from August to September.

Blender revenue declined by $359.94.

The West region (R04) experienced a $526.98 revenue decline.

West region Blender sales decreased from nine units in August to zero in September.

Recommendations

Investigate Blender inventory availability in the West region.

Review customer demand and promotional activity before introducing corrective measures.

Continue monitoring product and regional sales performance.

Limitations

The dataset does not include inventory availability or customer traffic information. One transaction also has a missing quantity, preventing a reliable revenue calculation for that record.
